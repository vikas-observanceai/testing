UPCOMING EVENT

Meet & Share Your Priorities

A casual outdoor conversation — you set the agenda.

Sunday, April 26, 2026 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM (ADT)
Lincoln Heights Park – Open Green Area 119 Goodine St, Fredericton, NB
Free & Open to All Families welcome · Light refreshments provided

Fredericton's City Councillor election is coming up, and I'm running to represent you in Ward 7.

Before you vote, let's talk.

Join me at Lincoln Heights Park for "Meet & Share Your Priorities" — a casual, outdoor event where you set the agenda. We've identified 5 key issues facing Ward 7. You'll see 5 boxes, one for each issue. Grab a sticker and "vote" for your top priority — then tell me why it matters to you.

The 5 Issues We're Tracking

1
Expand the Trail

Connecting neighbourhoods and keeping green space accessible.

2
Property Taxes & City Spending

Making sure tax hikes come with clear, accountable results.

3
Traffic Safety

Speeding, traffic lights, and safer crossings for kids and seniors.

4
City Services

Roads, sidewalks, snow clearing, and downtown safety.

5
Rehabilitation Centre & Addiction Supports

Access to treatment, mental health services, and community impact.

No speeches. No politics as usual. Just real conversations, light refreshments, and your voice shaping my priorities. Bring the kids, bring a neighbour, bring your thoughts. This is about listening first.
— Manisha Varma, Your Ward 7 Candidate
