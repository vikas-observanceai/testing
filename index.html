About the Candidate

Meet Manisha Varma

Manisha Varma is a dedicated community leader who has called Lincoln, New Brunswick home for over two decades. A mother, entrepreneur, and advocate, she has spent years working alongside residents to strengthen local neighbourhoods and ensure every voice is heard.

With a background in community development and small business, Manisha understands the challenges facing Lincoln families — from rising costs of living to the need for better infrastructure and services. She believes that strong communities are built from the ground up, with transparent leadership and real accountability.

Manisha's campaign is rooted in listening. She has held town halls across Lincoln, meeting with families, local business owners, seniors, and youth to craft a platform that reflects real needs — not political talking points.

Manisha's Platform for Lincoln

Six priorities. One community. A plan that delivers.

Community Safety

More resources for local policing, improved street lighting throughout Lincoln, and stronger partnerships between residents and emergency services.

Economic Growth

Support for small businesses and local entrepreneurs, attracting sustainable investment to Lincoln, and creating well-paying jobs for residents.

Infrastructure

Fixing roads and sidewalks, upgrading aging water and sewer systems, and investing in parks and recreational facilities for all ages.

Environment

Protecting Lincoln's natural spaces, expanding recycling and composting programs, and developing a local climate resilience plan.

Healthcare Access

Advocating for better access to family doctors and mental health services, and supporting community health initiatives for seniors and youth.

Education

Championing fully funded public schools, accessible childcare, and after-school programs that give Lincoln's children every opportunity to succeed.
