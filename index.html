About the Candidate

Meet Manisha Varma

Manisha Varma is a dedicated community leader running for Fredericton City Council in Ward 7 – Southwood Park Lincoln. A passionate advocate for inclusivity and community wellbeing, she has spent years building bridges across Fredericton's diverse neighbourhoods.

As a longtime resident of Ward 7, Manisha understands the real challenges facing families in Southwood Park and Lincoln — from housing affordability to accessible services for youth and seniors. She believes every resident deserves a seat at the table.

Recognized by the City of Fredericton for her community contributions, Manisha brings lived experience, genuine empathy, and a commitment to inclusive leadership to everything she does.

Election Platform

Manisha's Platform for Ward 7

Three pillars. One community. Real results for Southwood Park & Lincoln.

Empowering Community & Inclusivity

Building a Ward 7 where every voice is heard and every resident is valued — regardless of background, language, or circumstance. Manisha will champion inclusive programs and multicultural community spaces.

Supporting Families & Youth

Investing in accessible childcare, after-school programs, youth recreational spaces, and supports for seniors — so families in Southwood Park and Lincoln can thrive at every stage of life.

Advocating for Housing & Community Wellbeing

Pushing for affordable housing solutions, mental health resources, and neighbourhood infrastructure improvements that make Ward 7 a safe and welcoming place for all residents.
